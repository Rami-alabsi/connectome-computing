# Prior Art

This document prevents overclaiming novelty and will be updated as the project develops.

## 1. FlyWire Whole-Brain Connectome
Dorkenwald et al. published the adult Drosophila whole-brain wiring diagram in *Nature* (2024). The reconstruction reports 139,255 neurons and 54.5 million synapses.

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07558-y

## 2. Whole-Brain Network Analysis
Lin et al. analyzed the whole-brain graph and reported recurrent motifs, rich-club organization and other network properties.

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07968-y

## 3. Biological Processing Units
Yu et al. proposed Biological Processing Units derived from an insect connectome and evaluated the resulting recurrent architecture on machine-learning and chess-related tasks.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10951

**Implication:** converting an insect connectome into a computational network is not itself a novelty claim for this project.

## 4. Loihi 2 Fly Connectome
Wang et al. reported a full FlyWire connectome implementation on Loihi 2, covering approximately 140K neurons and 50M synapses on 12 chips.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16792

## 5. FlyWire Annotation and Cell Typing
Schlegel et al. provided hierarchical annotation and multi-connectome cell typing for FlyWire.

Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07686-5

## Working Novelty Hypothesis
The project investigates the combination of:
1. biological design-rule extraction;
2. explicit computational primitives;
3. controlled synthetic network generation;
4. multi-scale growth;
5. structural ablation and null models;
6. cross-scale benchmarking;
7. human-connectomics-informed constraints.

This is a working hypothesis, not a confirmed novelty claim.
